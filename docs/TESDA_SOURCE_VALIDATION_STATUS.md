## Confirmed structural facts

The reviewed Training Regulation and current SAG identify qualification code `ELCCSS213`. The TR identifies these four core units and maps the COC accumulation route to them:

1. `ELC724331` - Install and Configure Computer Systems.
2. `ELC724332` - Set-up Computer Networks.
3. `ELC724333` - Set-up Computer Servers.
4. `ELC724334` - Maintain and Repair Computer Systems and Networks.

TR Section 4.1 states that competence must be demonstrated in all units listed in Section 1. Section 4.2.1 maps accumulation of the four COCs to the four core units. These requirements are `TESDA_OFFICIAL_APPROVED`; they do not authorize ByteQuest to invent a numeric passing rule or claim certification authority.

Circular No. 18, s. 2015 corroborates the amended Training Regulations and the four core units, but it does not supply a numeric operational scoring rubric. The current combined SAG is a self-assessment/readiness checklist and critical-aspect guide, not a complete machine-executable grading formula.

All five scanned pages of Circular No. 18 were visually reviewed. Its assessment provisions require official CSS NC II assessment/certification to follow TESDA's competency-assessment Quality Procedures Manual and Section 4 of the TR, and require mandatory assessment of graduating students in UTPRAS-registered CSS NC II programs. These governance requirements are `TESDA_OFFICIAL_APPROVED`; they do not make ByteQuest an accredited assessment center or TESDA certification issuer.

## Policy values not found

Targeted text inspection found no authoritative project-ready values for:

- passing percentage;
- numeric competent/not-yet-competent cutoff;
- criterion weighting percentages;
- safety weighting percentage;
- per-COC point value;
- maximum attempt count, retry cooldown, prerequisite score, or automatic retry lockout;
- XP or gamification formula; or
- leaderboard formula.

The two occurrences of the word `percentage` in the Training Regulation are mathematical knowledge topics, not assessment cutoffs. The reviewed SAG files contain checklist criteria but no numeric scoring threshold.

5. A CSS instructor/adviser should still perform content acceptance of the rendered scenarios before the recorded defense walkthrough, especially where real physical workmanship cannot be sensed by a 2D simulation.
