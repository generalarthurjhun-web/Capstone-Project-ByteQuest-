## Decision boundary

All five study objectives have implementation and automated runtime evidence within the stated supplementary-platform scope. This does not authorize ByteQuest to issue TESDA certification or claim that simulated evidence replaces every required physical demonstration and assessor judgment. All 14 panel recommendations are complete with implementation and runtime evidence, including a real OpenRouter generation plus Instructor-controlled publication lifecycle. The remaining P0 evidence is a human-operated representative Mobile/Web acceptance journey, not missing competency architecture.
